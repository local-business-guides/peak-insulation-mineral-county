Questions to ask before scheduling

Use a few direct questions to understand the proposed work. Which parts of the building will be insulated? What material is being considered for those areas? How will the work account for air leakage? What should be moved or made accessible before the crew arrives? Clear answers help you compare plans based on the work described, rather than choosing by material name alone.

A little preparation can make that conversation easier:

  • Write down the rooms or building sections you want reviewed.
  • Describe when you notice drafts or uncomfortable temperatures.
  • Gather any information you have about earlier insulation work.
  • Ask what preparation is needed and what areas are included.

One mistake to avoid is assuming that a thicker layer will fix every comfort concern. Ask how sealing and insulation fit together for the areas being discussed.
